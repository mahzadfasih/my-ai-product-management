## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno assigns reach and impact score to workflows based on customer feedback in online forums. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## The harness

| Surface | Specification |
|---|---|
| 01 Context | the raw signals, the sales pipeline snapshot, and product strategy one pager so it has customer and business value context |
| 02 Tools | it can assign a score of 1 to 5 for reach and for impact for each of the given workflows. |
| 03 Loop | one pass per signal |
| 04 Memory | a summary of customer sentiment. if human edits the suggested score, capture and store the change. |
| 05 Permissions | read auto · draft auto · write auto · send confirm |
| 06 Verification | the PM will verify and approve. |

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

the raw signals, the sales pipeline snapshot, and product strategy one pager so it has customer and business value context

**How fresh must it be**

changes in context will change answers on monthly basis. if the source is unavailable,  rely on the online forum search only.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

it can assign a score of 1 to 5 for reach and for impact for each of the given workflows.

**Deliberate omissions**

it cannot draft or write workflows.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

one pass per signal

**Latency and cost target**

answers back in under 8 seconds 95% of times, and cost per task stays predictable

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

a summary of customer sentiment. if human edits the suggested score, capture and store the change.

**Expiry and write rules**

customer sentiment will be stored for two years. human corrections will be stored for 6 months. product manager's corrections will outrank other editors.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | auto |
| send | confirm |

**Justification**

it can draft and post reach and impact scores. it can't send to stakeholders without human confirmation.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

the PM will verify and approve.

**Failure behaviour**

post output with message that the validation has failed.
